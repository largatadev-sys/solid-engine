## Glossary — the ubiquitous language  *(register #3 resolved 2026-08-08 — S4.15 grilling: the Trip / Itinerary split ratified)*

| Term | Precise meaning |
|---|---|
| **Traveler (User)** | An authenticated account. The sole platform actor in v1. |
| **Handle (@username)** | The Traveler's unique, changeable public label (`@janedoe`): 3–20 chars, lowercase, globally unique. **A label, never a key** — ids remain the identifier everywhere, which is what makes free change safe (ADR-015). Distinct from **display name**, which stays non-unique. *(Added 2026-07-30, S4.0 grilling — supersedes the 07-17 "no handles in MVP" ruling.)* |
| **Visitor** | An unauthenticated reader. Strictly read-only (INV-3). Not an entity — an access level. |
| **Public Profile** | The projection of a Traveler any signed-in traveler may read: handle, display name, avatar, bio, vanity number, the published showcase, the shared postcards, and two counts (published itineraries · postcards). Not an entity — a projection. Exists for **every onboarded Traveler** — onboarding, not publishing, is the threshold; an un-onboarded account has none. Excludes by name: email, country, home city, preferred currency, goals, interests. Addressed by handle; identified by id (ADR-015). *(Added 2026-08-25, S4.36 grilling.)* |
| **People Search** | Finding Travelers from Discover by handle or display name — never by email, never without a query (two characters minimum), never as a browsable list. The enumeration fences are part of the definition, not an implementation detail. *(Added 2026-08-25, S4.36 grilling.)* |
| **Trip** | The traveler's journey-object through its whole lifecycle — the word every own-surface uses (the Trips tab, trip cards, **Trip Workspace**, Plan a Trip, Create Trip). A Trip *has* an Itinerary and never stops being a trip. UI language only — code, routes, entities and the wire stay `Itinerary`. *(Ratified 2026-08-08, S4.15 grilling — closes register #3. The original rationale's crystallizing act — "Finish Planning turns the working plan into a finished itinerary" — retired at ADR-029; the split itself stands unchanged.)* |
| **Itinerary** | The plan: a standalone, forkable, publishable object owning Days and their Activities. In traveler-facing copy it names **the plan document a Trip carries** — the thing publishing makes discoverable/forkable *(Finish Planning retired at ADR-029)*; discovery surfaces say "itinerary", own surfaces say "trip". *(Split ratified 2026-08-08, S4.15 grilling.)* |
| **Destination** | **Exactly one per Itinerary** — the place the trip is about, free text, the string cards, trending buckets, discovery filters and the published pill all read. *(Founder ruling 2026-08-09; the plural wire narrowed to a scalar at S4.25/ADR-028, backfilled keep-first. A trip that visited several places names the one it was **about** — the plan's Days carry the rest.)* Distinct from **Place**, an Activity's own free-text location. |
| **Trip Currency** | **Exactly one per Itinerary** — the money every activity price on that trip is labelled in. Born `PHP` (server-side default, S4.25/ADR-028), changed only by the owner through the details editor. Changing it **relabels** every priced activity in one transaction: the numbers are untouched and **no FX is ever applied** — ₱1,500 becomes $1,500, and the traveler is told so before it happens. Distinct from the Traveler's **preferred currency**, which keeps its one meaning as the E5 expense-logging default and never seeds this. |
| **Day** | One ordinal slot of the plan (Day 1…N), optionally titled ("Arrival & Sunsets"). Owns its Activities and their order. Plans are **day-indexed, not date-anchored** (ADR-013); when the itinerary has dates, a Day's calendar date is derived, never stored. *(Added at the S1.3 grilling, 2026-07-23.)* |
| **Activity** | One element of a Day's plan — transport, meal, stay, sight, anything scheduled. The name reads narrower than it means, deliberately *(renamed from **Itinerary Item** at the S1.3 grilling, 2026-07-23 — founder call: the UI says "Activity" and canon follows; the wire noun is permanent within /v1, accepted knowingly in ADR-013)*. Carries a `source`. |
| **Fork** | *(verb)* To take a plan-only copy of a published Itinerary into a new Trip of one's own — one tap, collecting nothing. The copy carries the plan (title verbatim, destination, description, standouts, best time of year, Trip Currency, every Day in order, every Activity with its time, place, description, Creator Tips, booking facts and cost) and **nothing else**: no dates, no cover, no photo, no member, no history, no stamp, and none of the source's social record (INV-6, ADR-013, ADR-017). It is born `upcoming` *(ADR-029; `draft` at build)*, unpublished, with the forker as sole owner. **Availability is exactly view availability** — anyone who passes the published audience fence may fork, the author and the source's own members included; every refusal is the fence's own 404. Re-forking is allowed and dedupes nothing: a second run of the trip is a fresh copy. *(Built at S4.7, 2026-08-19.)* |
| **Fork Relationship** | Provenance record: this Itinerary was copied from that one. **Exactly one hop**: the row names the Itinerary the traveler tapped Fork on, never a chain's root — so a fork of a fork credits its immediate source. Permanent and append-only; unpublishing or archiving a source deletes nobody's fork and never decrements its count. *(One-hop rule and the identity note added at S4.7, 2026-08-19.)* |
| **Trip Workspace** | The private collaboration space around one Itinerary: membership, decisions, ledger. *(Comments removed 2026-07-24 — see **Comment**.)* Since S4.17 also the space's **home surface**: the screen every own unpublished trip opens into, in every lifecycle state — read-only presentation under the lifecycle ladder; editing lives in the **Itinerary Workspace**. Published trips open their published view, never the workspace. *(Surface meaning added 2026-08-08, S4.17 grilling; the editor renamed at ADR-027.)* |
| **Itinerary Workspace** | The plan's editing surface — the screen behind **Edit Itinerary**, where the day-by-day accordion builds Days and Activities. It opens **in place, from every unpublished, unarchived lifecycle state, for any member** — entering costs no state *(ADR-027, 2026-08-15, retired the S4.17 reopen-on-entry soft lock, and renamed the surface from **Draft Workspace**: it no longer implies the draft state, and the name pairs with the Trip/Itinerary split — the Trip Workspace is the journey's home, the Itinerary Workspace edits the plan document it carries)*. Only the **Editing Session** holder edits; **Save Changes** commits the staged plan, ends the session, and returns to the Trip Workspace. *(Added 2026-08-08, S4.17 grilling; saving semantics amended 2026-08-10, S4.18 — ADR-023 reversed ADR-022's "persists nothing" after the founder pressed back and found an unsaved reorder already saved.)* **Its badge copy is "Trip Workspace", in every state** — the surface canon names are unchanged, but the chip a traveler reads while editing says where they *are*, while the viewer's chip says where the *trip* is (Upcoming / Ongoing / Completed — the states' own names since ADR-029). *(Badge copy amended 2026-08-09, S4.19 decision 1 — founder held it twice over this collision; generalized to every state at ADR-027.)* |
| **Editing Session** | The exclusive whole-itinerary hold one traveler has while in the Itinerary Workspace, and the **staging context** their edits accumulate in: acquired by Edit Itinerary, ended by **Save Changes** or by discarding on the way out, self-expiring when abandoned. The seven plan ops mutate a local draft of the plan document — nothing reaches the server until the commit — so other members read the last *saved* plan for the session's whole length ("being edited by …"). Restores ADR-014's original whole-plan scope as a deliberate session, superseding the subject-typed leases — chosen because live co-editing sync does not exist (ADR-022). **Its boundaries broadcast** (S4.35): acquiring and releasing a session are pushed to the trip's other members, so the advisory indicator moves while they are looking at it rather than when they next refetch. This changes when they learn, not what they read — the plan inside the session is still the last *saved* plan, and **a broadcast session boundary is not live co-editing sync**; ADR-022's invalidating condition has not fired. *(Added 2026-08-08; staging meaning added 2026-08-10, S4.18 — ADR-023; broadcast meaning added 2026-08-24, S4.35 — ADR-030's amendment.)* |
| **Save Changes** | The commit act that ends an Editing Session: the staged plan lands via one transactional, version-checked bulk write, the session releases, and the Trip Workspace returns. With nothing staged it is a plain exit. Leaving any other way confirms first, then discards the staging — what was never saved never happened, which is also why staged-then-undone churn leaves no history. *(Added 2026-08-10, S4.18 — ADR-023.)* |
| **Plan Version** | The revision number of an Itinerary's plan document, bumped by **every** write that mutates it — the bulk save and each still-live per-action endpoint, so an old client's write stays visible to the check. A bulk save carries the version its staging began from; a stale base refuses rather than overwriting blindly, and the traveler chooses explicitly between discarding and re-basing. It tracks the plan alone: reads, lifecycle acts, publish, trip-field edits and cover uploads never bump it. *(Added 2026-08-10, S4.18 — ADR-023.)* |
| **Membership** | A Traveler's role in one Workspace: `owner` or `member`. |
| **Ownership Offer** | The owner's pending proposal to hand their Workspace's ownership to a named member. At most one pending per Workspace; accepting it executes the transfer (INV-4). Ownership moves by consent, never imposition. *(S1.6, 2026-07-28.)* |
| **Poll / Vote** | A question put to the trip on the Workspace's voting board (where to eat, which hotel) — **free-standing**: attached to nothing in the plan, writing nothing back to it. Single-choice; one vote per member per poll (INV-10), attributed and visible to every member, changeable while open. Closes at its required deadline or early by its creator or the owner; the winner is the highest count, computed — ties carry every leader. *(Renamed from **Decision** and ruled free-standing at the E2 grilling, 2026-08-20 — the founders' E2 UX-flow discussion the epic map named as this epic's trigger; S2.1.)* |
| **Comment** | Public discussion attached to a **published** Itinerary — one meaning only (rules `OPEN → register #5`; builds at S4.6, after publish exists). **There are no planning/private comments** *(removed 2026-07-24, S1.4 grilling — founder ruling: trip groups coordinate in their own channels; in-app private discussion duplicates them without value)*. |
| **Review** | A post-trip assessment attached to a published Itinerary. Rules `OPEN → register #4`. |
| **Star / Upvote** | A lightweight reaction on a published Itinerary. |
| **Diary** | One traveler's collection of Diary Entries for one trip — a projection over the entries, not a stored entity *(re-modeled 2026-08-11, ADR-024 — supersedes the shared-album "one author-owner, consented contributors" definition; contributor grants are deleted)*. One itinerary → many diaries, exactly one per traveler. Public from the moment an entry is posted (S4.22, ADR-025 amended) — the diary itself is still one traveler's, and only its author writes. |
| **Diary Entry** | One postcard in a Diary: derived from one Activity (at most one entry per traveler per activity), carrying 1–5 photos and an optional caption, **snapshotting** the activity's title/day/time at post time — provenance nullable, plan edits never rewrite it (ADR-024). Free-standing entries and the geotag are parked. |
| **Highlight** | A published Diary as surfaced on its published Itinerary. A projection, not a separate entity. **Never** the creator-listed selling points on a published plan — those are **Standouts**. |
| **Standout** | A creator-listed selling point on a published Itinerary ("Big Lagoon Kayaking"): an ordered list of short free-text strings, edited under the header lease, rendered on the published Overview. **Builds at S4.1** *(term reserved at the S1.3 grilling, 2026-07-23, because the 07/18 mock labeled these "Trip Highlights", colliding with **Highlight** above)*. |
| **Gallery** | The published Overview's photo grid: a **derived projection** aggregating the Itinerary's cover and its Activities' photos. A projection, never an entity — an itinerary-level photo set is deliberately absent, because **Diary** is already the album concept and a second album would collide with it. *(Added 2026-08-06, S3.3 grilling, founder-ruled.)* |
| **Photo** | An uploaded image attached to a Traveler (avatar), an Itinerary (cover), an Activity, the trip's **Photo Dump**, or a **Diary Entry** *(the last two added 2026-08-11, S3.4/S3.1)*. Bytes live in the object store, metadata in Postgres (ADR-002); reached only through backend media URLs that enforce the attachment's audience (ADR-021); stripped of embedded metadata at ingest (INV-11); never crosses a fork (INV-6). The **Gallery** is its derived projection. Video is not a Photo — it is a parked premium capability (register #14). *(Added 2026-08-06, S3.3 grilling.)* |
| **Photo Dump** | The trip's member-uploaded shared photo pool, on the workspace tab of the same name — **uploads only, never derived content** *(founder-ruled 2026-08-11, S3.4 grilling: "it's just a photo dump"; the tab's earlier Gallery association retires — Gallery keeps its published-side derived-projection meaning)*. Any member uploads; uploader deletes their own, the owner any; workspace-walled (INV-1). A dump photo selected into a Diary Entry is **copied** — dump deletion never reaches a diary. |
| **Ledger** | The Workspace's money record: Expenses, Splits, Transfers. Append-only (INV-8). |
| **Expense** | A cost incurred on the trip, split across members (INV-7). Contributes to trip total. |
| **Transfer** | A settlement / waiver / reassignment between members. Changes balances, never the trip total. |
| **Invitation** | **The trip asking a traveler** into its Workspace — by exact @handle (S4.9) or, historically, by email (the email path's UI retired at S4.28; the endpoint stays dormant under ADR-008). The invitee consents by accepting; no owner action. Issuable by **any member** since S4.28/ADR-032 (owner-only from S1.2 until then — the additive widening that grilling anticipated). One of the **two consent directions** — its mirror is the **Join Request**. *(Redefined 2026-08-22, S4.28 grilling.)* |
| **Invite Link** | The trip's single shareable join token (S4.28/ADR-032): one live link per Workspace, opaque and unguessable, minted lazily, reused forever — no expiry, no regeneration (reset parked with its trigger). Holding it grants **a teaser and the right to ask** — never entry: opening it shows the trip's postcard landing, and joining goes through a **Join Request** the owner approves. The link belongs to the trip, not a person — the landing never names an inviter. Dead while the trip is archived or published. *(Added 2026-08-22, S4.28 grilling.)* |
| **Join Request** | **A traveler asking the trip** (S4.28/ADR-032): created from the invite-link landing by a signed-in, onboarded, email-verified traveler; the owner consents by approving — which creates the membership immediately, the request having been the requester's consent — or declines silently (retryable). The mirror of **Invitation**; membership arriving by either direction supersedes the other's pending rows. *(Added 2026-08-22, S4.28 grilling.)* |
| **Discovery** | The surface for browsing/searching **published Itineraries** (E4; UX flow 5). Never means finding places or activities — that is Place Search. *(Added 2026-07-17, UX reconciliation — the two were colliding in conversation.)* |
| **Place Search** | *Reserved term, future phase (register #9):* searching destinations/POIs **inside the app** to build a plan. Not the unfurler (which enriches a link the traveler already found elsewhere) and not in MVP scope. |
| **Active (workspace)** | The `WorkspaceState` value a live (non-archived) trip holds — what the workspace screen's status chip renders (S4.9). *(Added 2026-07-31, trip-surfaces reconciliation, when it collided with the itinerary-lifecycle `active`; ADR-020 renamed that state `ongoing`, dissolving the collision — the workspace value keeps the word.)* *(The word partially returned at ADR-027: the lifecycle's `ongoing` rendered as "Active" — label only. **Dissolved again at ADR-029, 2026-08-20:** the lifecycle label retires, `ongoing` presents as "Ongoing", and "Active" once more means only this workspace value.)* |
| **Upcoming** | The lifecycle's **birth state** (ADR-029): planning happens here — creation and fork both land on it, and no terminal declaration exists. The ladder's floor: `reopen` refuses here with the named 409. Editable like every unpublished state — **Edit Itinerary** opens the Itinerary Workspace in place (ADR-027, upheld); **Start Trip**, confirmed in its drawer, moves it to `ongoing`. Presents under its own name: **Upcoming**. *(History: added as the planning-finished state at ADR-020, 2026-08-04; labelled "Ready" from S4.17; ADR-029, 2026-08-20, deleted `draft`, retired Finish Planning and the Ready label, and made this the birth state.)* |
| **Ongoing** | The lifecycle state while the trip is being lived (ADR-020; renames ADR-019's `active`). Entered by the traveler's tap — **Start Trip**, confirmed in its drawer — never by dates. Presents under its own name everywhere: **Ongoing** *(ADR-029 retired ADR-027's "Active" label; see **Active (workspace)**)*. *(Added 2026-08-04; Active label 2026-08-15, retired 2026-08-20.)* |
| **Finish Planning** *(retired)* | **Retired at ADR-029 (2026-08-20)** — `draft` deleted, so the act has no edge to travel; the endpoint stays on the wire, permanently refusing (ADR-008 dormancy). Kept for the naming argument it carried: one word must not name both *done planning* and *the trip happened* (ADR-018's argument, third application) — which now guards the transition drawers' wording instead. *(Original: the creation flow's terminal act, `draft → upcoming`; UI "Finalize Itinerary" from S4.17.)* |
| **In-trip Chat** | The workspace-scoped conversation among a trip's members (S4.10): one text thread per trip, live over the WS-1 socket (ADR-030), messages append-only (≤ 2,000 chars, no edit/delete/threading), attributed to the **traveler** so they survive the author's departure (the deliberate divergence from votes-die-with-the-membership). **A planning surface: open in every unpublished lifecycle state; publish closes it** (named refusal; unpublish reopens, history intact); archive renders it read-only under S4.23's posture. Never crosses the publish wall (INV-1 — workspace-grade, like the roster); writes no activity history. Not the deleted private Comment (2026-07-24 — stays deleted) and not the public Comment (S4.6). *(Entered launch scope 2026-07-31; grilled 2026-08-20 — three rounds; photos-in-chat raised and withdrawn in the same session, dismissed on the record.)* |
| **Visibility (itinerary)** | One of the itinerary's **three orthogonal axes** (ADR-019): `public` (every onboarded traveler may read) or `private` (owner + collaborators — INV-1), toggleable any time, independent of the feed. The other two axes: **lifecycle** `state` (`upcoming → ongoing → completed`, ADR-020 as re-cut by ADR-029) and **discovery** `published` (boolean — the feed and the freeze; publishing requires `completed` and is the owner's act). `friends_only` never becomes a tier — the friend graph narrows `public`. Archive still dominates everything: an archived trip is owner-only-visible. *(Reworked 2026-08-03/04, ADR-019/020; original binary entry was ADR-017's.)* |
| **Creator Tips** | The Activity's `notes` field, re-semanticized at S4.1 *(2026-07-31, founder-ruled — supersedes S1.3's private planning semantics; ADR-008 waiver)*: per-activity guidance for the trip itself ("book the 8 AM slot"), **public when the itinerary publishes, copied on fork** (plan data under INV-6). Distinct from **planning notes** — private member coordination, never built, parked on the epic-map backlog (S4.10's chat likely covers it). |
| **Spec** *(harness)* | One test file in the Playwright suite (`mobile/e2e/`), owned by a runner and a project. **The web rung has specs, not walks** — the term `walk` retires from it entirely at H1 and survives only on the device rung, where it names a genuinely different activity: a human or `adb` driving a build on an emulator. Saying "walk" about the web rung now names something that no longer exists. *(H1, 2026-08-18; ADR-026.)* |
| **Walk** *(harness)* | A scripted or hand-driven pass over the **device** rung — an emulator or a physical handset. The only rung where the word still applies. |
| **Project** *(harness)* | One of the suite's two halves: `api` (request-context, no browser) and `web` (phone viewport with touch). A gate report reads per project. |
| **The gate** | The three rungs a promotion is proposed on: the Playwright suite, the backend integration tests, and the emulator walk. The founder's physical-device pass sits on top and remains theirs. |

## High-level flow — the journey (per actor)

**Traveler (organizer):** sign up → create Itinerary (scratch or fork, born `upcoming` — ADR-029) → invite co-travelers → Workspace forms → collaborate (days + activities, decisions — plan editing is an exclusive per-itinerary **Editing Session** in the Itinerary Workspace; ADR-022, superseding ADR-014's subject-typed leases; in-place from every unpublished state since ADR-027) → trip runs (**Start Trip**, drawer-confirmed → `ongoing`; diary + expenses accrete) → **Complete Trip**, drawer-confirmed → **publish to the feed — only from `completed`** *(ADR-019/020: the feed carries records of trips that happened; publishing freezes the plan)* → reviews.

**Traveler (co-traveler):** receive email invite → authenticate → land in Workspace as member → collaborate, vote, log expenses, contribute to consented diaries → review after completion.

**Traveler (consumer):** browse/discover published Itineraries → view plan, Highlights, aggregate cost → star, comment, review → **fork** → become an organizer. *The loop closes: consumption feeds creation.*

**Diary author (any member):** trip starts (`ongoing`) → pick an activity on Day-by-Day → **Add to Diary** (1–5 photos from device or the Photo Dump + a caption; the activity's title/day/time snapshot into the postcard) → the entry lands in *their own* per-trip Diary, on their profile, and on the public Home feed the instant it is posted (S4.22, ADR-025 amended) → retro-posting continues after `completed`. *(Re-drawn 2026-08-11, ADR-024 — supersedes the shared-album flow: no diary creation act, no contributor grants, no publish act yet. Diary publish + Highlights build at S4.2, which inherits register #13 and the dump-photo consent must-answer; the pre-publish surface is ruled — the profile.)*

**Visitor:** view published content, including reviews and comments. Nothing else (INV-3). *(Unlisted deleted at S4.1 — visibility is binary; accountless reach itself arrives with the backlogged web read-only surface, until which "the public" means signed-in Travelers.)*

The forkable/publishable unit. |
| **Day** | id, itinerary id, ordinal (contiguous 1…N — deletion renumbers), optional title | One slot of the day-indexed plan (ADR-013). When the itinerary has dates, Day N's calendar date is *derived* (start + N−1), never stored. *(S1.3, 2026-07-23.)* |
| **Activity** | id, day id, sort order *(manual, authoritative — time is display metadata)*, title, optional time-of-day *(local, timezone-free)*, optional estimated cost (amount + currency — **creator-stated, uninterpreted**: not per-person, not verified; the meaning is the creator's own framing — S4.1; **absent means "not stated", never free — free is an explicit 0**, rendered "Free"; the form's prefilled home currency is a hint that becomes data only once an amount is typed — ADR-027), place *(free text in v1 — geotag arrives with Place Search, epic-map backlog)*, description, notes *(**Creator Tips** — public on publish, copied on fork; re-semanticized at S4.1, 2026-07-31, superseding S1.3's private planning semantics; ADR-008 waiver — see glossary)*, **one optional booking link** *(the URL is the S0.3 `externalUrl`; joined 2026-08-04 by additive nullable purpose, provider, price amount + currency — the drawn card whole, founder-ruled at the create-flow grilling. ## Aggregates & consistency boundaries

- **Itinerary aggregate** — root: Itinerary; inside: Days and Activities. **Strong consistency within** (day-ordinal contiguity, activity ordering, edits). Collaborative editing is **leased per subject — activity / day / itinerary header** (ADR-014 as amended 2026-07-31 at the trip-surfaces reconciliation; originally whole-itinerary, 2026-07-24 — both supersede the 07-17 last-write-wins ruling that S1.3 shipped), with adds unguarded, reorder version-checked, and `last edited by/at` attribution on Itinerary and Activity retained (S1.3). Live editing is the declared post-gate replacement. Carries visibility, lifecycle state, fork lineage. Exposes: **published aggregate cost** — a *derived value* computed from its Workspace's ledger (**expenses only, transfers excluded**), live-derived (a late expense updates it), and **the only ledger fact that ever crosses the boundary** (INV-2).
- **Trip Workspace aggregate** — root: Workspace; inside: Memberships, Invitations, Ownership Offers + Ownership Transfer records (S1.6), Polls+Votes *(Decisions renamed, 2026-08-20 — S2.1; Comments removed 2026-07-24 — Comment is public-only, attached to published Itineraries, S4.6)*, and the **Ledger as a bounded module** (Expenses, Splits, Transfers — own tables, own service interface, touched only through it; promotable to its own aggregate/service in the payments phase by addition). **Strong consistency within** — INV-4, 7, 8, 10 are enforced here, transactionally.
- **Diary aggregate** — root: Diary; inside: Contributor Grants, Diary Entries. Owner-consistent (INV-2a, 5). **References** its Itinerary by ID — never contained by it.
- **Cross-aggregate rules:** references by ID only; eventual consistency across (stars, feed, discovery counts may lag seconds — recorded in 01's NFRs). Reviews, Stars, and Comments attach to *published* Itineraries. Fork copies Itinerary-aggregate data only (INV-6).

## Invariants (normative — restated from Artifact 00 §4, attached to their owning aggregate)

**Workspace aggregate**
- **INV-1.** Only members view/modify a non-published Workspace's contents; an **archived** Workspace narrows further, to the owner alone *(the audience ladder — S4.1/ADR-017, 2026-07-31)*.
- **INV-4.** Exactly one owner at all times; ownership transfers or is claimed — never vanishes.
- **INV-7.** Σ(splits) = expense total, always, transactionally.
- **INV-8.** Ledger is append-only: corrections/waivers/settlements/reassignments are new Transfer entries. Nothing silently edited or deleted.
- **INV-10.** One vote per member per poll; members only. *(Reworded at the Decision → Poll rename, 2026-08-20 — S2.1.)*

**Itinerary aggregate**
- **INV-2.** Publishing exposes: the plan (Creator Tips included), its publish metadata (Standouts, best time, cover), its published diaries (Highlights), creator-stated estimated costs with their derived single-currency total, aggregate trip cost (E5), and **the owner's public identity** — the byline, resolved to the *current* owner at render (S4.1). Never: ledger detail, raw diaries, the roster, per-field edit attribution — **and never anything that reveals current or future absence: no absolute dates, no lifecycle state, no lifecycle stamps** *(the absence rule — S4.1/ADR-017; binds every future projection surface: diary-entry recency on the published page inherits it, register #13. **Scope pinned 2026-08-12, ADR-025:** the rule binds the plan's projection — involuntary exposure; a postcard the author deliberately posted carries its recency legally, identifying no one but its author, since no public surface carries the roster. *Amended 2026-08-13: the deliberate act is now posting rather than sharing — the clause holds with the one word swapped, because posting a diary entry remains a thing the author chose to do*)*.
- **INV-6.** Every fork records provenance; forked content is plan data only — **and media never crosses**: photos and the cover are excluded from the copy, so a fork starts photo-less *(founder-ruled at the S3.3 grilling, 2026-08-06; Creator Tips still cross — words fork, images do not)*.

**Diary aggregate** *(both amended 2026-08-11, ADR-024 — the postcard model; contributor grants deleted)*
- **INV-2a.** A diary has exactly one author; **only the author writes**. **Every diary entry is public from the moment it is posted** *(S4.22, ADR-025 as amended 2026-08-13 — decision 1's per-entry opt-in reversed by the founder before the merge: "they default to public")*. There is no per-entry visibility act, no toggle and no unshare; posting **is** publishing. Retraction comes at two grains: **delete** one postcard (permanent), or **archive the trip**, which withdraws all of its postcards from the feed at once and closes its public diary to 404 — reversible by unarchiving *(amended 2026-08-13; the feed had been withholding an archived trip's link while still showing its cards)*. The entry reads nothing about a photo's origin — contributing to the trip's Photo Dump consents to co-travelers' postcard use, public included. Diary-level publication, when it exists (S4.2), is sugar over this and adds no privacy state. *(Trip-level `visibility` is a different axis and does not gate diary entries — ADR-019.)*
- **INV-5.** Every Diary belongs to one traveler and one Itinerary; every entry derives from at most one Activity of that itinerary (max one entry per traveler per activity), snapshotting its title/day/time at post time — provenance nullable, never rewritten by plan edits.

**Platform-wide**
- **INV-3.** Visitors are strictly read-only; all interaction requires an account.
- **INV-11.** All uploaded media is re-encoded at ingest and carries **no embedded metadata** — EXIF/GPS/XMP never reach the store. The ingest-side sibling of INV-2's absence rule: a photo's GPS tag broadcasts a traveler's location more precisely than dates ever did, and location context belongs to the plan's own free-text fields. *(S3.3 grilling, 2026-08-06.)*

## State machines

### Itinerary
**Three orthogonal axes** *(ADR-019, 2026-08-03; the lifecycle ladder amended to four states by ADR-020, 2026-08-04, and **re-cut to three by ADR-029, 2026-08-20** — this section's earlier ADR-017 text, including the "dormant lifecycle" warning, is superseded: the lifecycle has a live UI surface again)*: **lifecycle** `state`, **discovery** `published`, **visibility** `public | private`.

**Lifecycle** `upcoming → ongoing → completed` *(ADR-029 — `draft` deleted; `upcoming` is the birth state)* — every transition the traveler's tap, never date-derived; a date-holding trip may nudge (pull-based, client-side — register #10's resolution survives). **UI labels: the states' own names** — Upcoming · Ongoing · Completed; "Ready" and "Active" retired at ADR-029, and the label drift ADR-027 priced is gone with them (wire and storage spellings never moved). The forward transitions confirm in drawers; one-step undo is API-only, flooring at `upcoming`; two-step jumps are refused. The Trips surface presents the ladder as three fixed tabs (Upcoming | Ongoing | Completed).

| From → To | Trigger |
|---|---|
| upcoming → ongoing | **Start Trip** *(drawer-confirmed; stamps `started-at`, write-once)* |
| ongoing → completed | **Complete Trip** *(drawer-confirmed; stamps `completed-at`, write-once)* |
| one step back | **`reopen`** — API-only since ADR-029 (the Step back UI retired; no undo affordance, accepted on the record); floors at `upcoming` (named 409); refused while published (the lifecycle is pinned) |

**Discovery** — `published` boolean; governs the feed **and the freeze**:

| From → To | Trigger |
|---|---|
| unpublished → published | **Owner publishes — requires `completed`** (ADR-019/020: the feed carries records of trips that happened); refused while archived (the S1.9 fence); defaults visibility `public` |
| published → unpublished | **Owner unpublishes** — thaws the plan, frees the lifecycle; the page masks to not-found; social objects hide, never delete; forks survive |

**Visibility** — `public ⇄ private`, toggleable any time in either direction, published or not; never changes `published` or `state`. `published + private` is legal: in the feed, readable only by owner + collaborators (the feed filters per viewer).

**Semantics:** the freeze belongs to `published` **alone** — no lifecycle state locks the plan: `upcoming` stays editable (Edit Itinerary opens the Itinerary Workspace in place from every unpublished state — ADR-027, upheld by ADR-029) and unpublished `completed` stays editable (completion is when the record gets corrected; the freeze's ground is readers, and readers arrive at publish). The stamps are write-once facts recording the owner's *act*, never on the projection (INV-2's absence rule holds). Birth is not a transition: creation bears `upcoming` (ADR-029); a `completed`-at-birth door is parked (ADR-020 — its birth-state parameter re-shapes to `upcoming | completed`).
**Illegal:** lifecycle jumps (`upcoming → completed` forward, `completed → upcoming` back, …) · publishing from `upcoming` or `ongoing` · any lifecycle transition while published · `draft` on any wire response (deleted at ADR-029; `category=draft` alone stays accepted-and-empty).

### Trip Workspace
**States:** `active → completed → archived` — active from creation (formation is atomic with the itinerary, S1.1). *(Register #12 resolved 2026-07-20 at S1.2's grilling: `forming` is collapsed — no behavior anywhere branches on it (INV-1 gates on membership, not state), and every backfilled pre-E1 workspace is solo-owner yet actively in use, so it would have been born in a factually wrong state.)* *(The `state` column deferred S1.2 → S1.7 → S1.9 and **ships at S1.9** — the archive story, its first true reader; the recursive deferral ends. S1.9 grilling, 2026-07-28.)*

| From → To | Trigger |
|---|---|
| active → completed | Mirrors the itinerary completing *(the mirror is written by the completion transaction, live from S1.9; S1.9 also backfills pre-existing completed trips)* |
| active / completed → archived | **Owner's explicit act only — never automatic.** *(S1.9: archivable from any itinerary state — the "skipping completed is illegal" line was amended, because a cancelled `upcoming` trip is archive's single most likely real use *(said of `draft` at S1.9; the state was remapped into `upcoming` at ADR-029, and the argument is unchanged)*.)* |
| archived → active / completed | **Unarchive — owner's explicit act.** Restores the state derivable from the itinerary (`completed` ↔ `COMPLETED`, else `ACTIVE`); no "previous state" is stored. *(S1.9, 2026-07-28 — supersedes "unarchive is deliberately absent from v1": archive became the **only** end-of-life act when delete parked, so a one-way freeze would make a mis-tap total rather than cosmetic, with psql the only recourse. Unarchive and the write fence are a package.)* |

**Semantics:** `completed` is the **working afterlife** — ledger still accepts Transfers (post-trip settling), reviews get written, diaries publish. `archived` freezes **acts on the trip** — plan edits, lifecycle, invitations, offers, removal of others, **publish/unpublish (S4.1)** — while **acts on one's own membership survive**: a member can always leave (founder ruling, S1.9 grilling: the line is a rule, not an exception — your relationship to the trip stays yours). **Archived also hides the trip from everyone but the owner** *(the audience ladder — S4.1/ADR-017, 2026-07-31, superseding S1.9's "archive evicts nobody" **sight** while the memberships themselves stay untouched)*: members' lists exclude it, their reads mask to not-found, and a published trip's public page goes down — **archive dominates publish**. Unarchive restores a working trip **and the prior audience, the public page included**. Self-leave stays permitted on the wire (ADR-008) but is unreachable from UI while hidden — accepted on the record. Pending invitations and ownership offers are `voided` transactionally at archive and never resurrected. The owner cannot leave an archived trip (INV-4, transfer-first — and transfer is frozen too): the path is unarchive → transfer.
**Illegal:** skipping a state on the forward edges · archive/unarchive by anyone but the owner.

### Diary
**One stored instant, per entry, and no state to choose** *(ADR-025 as amended 2026-08-13 — supersedes the `private ⇄ shared` pair minted a day earlier)*: an entry carries `shared_at`, stamped at construction, so **every entry is public the moment it exists** regardless of where its photos came from (dump contribution implies consent to co-travelers' postcard use — ADR-025 decision 2, unchanged). Any signed-in traveler reads the entry's feed card and its photos. The instant is what the feed **orders** on, which is why it is an instant and not a flag; the column stays nullable because the parked mechanism — the friend graph, or a later per-entry control — re-enters through it, but nothing writes NULL and no endpoint clears it. Every diary write is still archive-fenced, and delete is the only retraction. **Diary-level** state still does not exist and arrives additively at **S4.2** (Highlights — re-pointed 2026-08-12: diary-level publish, if still wanted, is sugar over per-entry shares; its inherited consent must-answer is discharged by ADR-025's dump-implies-consent ruling). Entry creation stays lifecycle-gated (`ongoing`/`completed` only — "once it is started"); sharing is independent of the **trip's** publish/lifecycle axes by design (the feed shows trips as they are lived).
