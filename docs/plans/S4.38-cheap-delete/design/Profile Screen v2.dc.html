Two screens, two ways to remove
Trips are swiped, everything inside a trip is kebabbed. The Trips screen is pulled from the shipped one — tabs, rows, Plan a Trip bar and all — with the swipe added to it. Note the two palettes meeting here: Trips runs on navy and terracotta, Profile on orange.
Reused, not rebuilt
The sheet is members/BottomSheet.tsx — 36×4 grabber, 17/22 title, travelerRadii.sheet 20, scrim rgba(28,25,23,0.4), swipe-dismiss at 60px. Entries follow MenuEntryRow: 19px glyph, 15/20 w600, 14/20 padding, destructive #B91C1C, MenuDivider between.
The undo toast is the house toast widened to hold an action: 14px radius, 13/17 w600 label, a hairline divider, a 44px Undo target, and a 2px line draining left to right over the window. No confirm drawer on this screen.
The kebab is moreHorizontal at 20px in #A59E99, in a 44px hit area — the same glyph, size and colour MemberRow already uses.
Menus
Diary — Edit diary details · Copy public link
Postcard — Edit postcard · Delete postcard
Itinerary — Edit details · View published page · Unpublish
A diary has no delete and needs none — it is the collection of its postcards. Delete the last one and the diary card collapses out of the list behind it, so an empty diary is never shown. Undo brings both back. Removing an itinerary from the world is unpublish, not delete: the trip survives untouched, so the word has to say so.
One rule for destructive acts
The weight of the confirm follows the blast radius, not the word delete. A sheet is for choosing; a modal is for stopping. Never both at once.
Undo
One item, your own — delete a postcard. No confirm. The row collapses and the toast holds Undo for 5s; the DELETE is held client-side until the window closes, so undo costs no round trip.
Undo
Reversible — unpublish the itinerary. No confirm either: it is a state, not a loss. Toast offers Republish.
Modal
Cascading, other people's data — delete a trip. Swipe the card, tap Delete, then a centre-screen modal with an acknowledgement to tick. Owner only; a member's swipe says Leave instead.
Nothing in the app confirms twice. The two-drawer stack is gone: a sheet is a menu, a modal is a wall, and a toast is the safety net after the fact.
Trips, as built
The Trips screen is app/(tabs)/(trips)/trips.tsx as it stands: 28/34 title with the coming-soon search glyph, TripTabRow across Upcoming · Ongoing · Completed on a 44px row with the 3px #D96C4A underline, TripRow cards — 76px cover, 15/20 title, destination · days subline, live-edit advisory with its pulsing dot, publication pill in accentTint.
Plan a Trip stays a 51px bar at 4px radius on Upcoming only; Archived trips stays an underlined link on Completed only; empty tabs keep their own copy. It opens on Ongoing, as landingTab does.
The toast is FeedToast geometry — pill, #1C1917, 13/17 semibold, 32px inset — widened to carry the Undo. That widening is the one component change this screen asks for.
Note for the API: ItineraryResponse carries no owner or role, so the list can't tell whose trip it is — and the swipe has to choose between Delete and Leave before it opens. The rows need a viewer role on the trips payload.
The swipe
Drag a trip card left to reveal a 96px action panel; it snaps open past half and springs back under. Only one card stays open at a time, and switching tabs closes it.
On first arrival the top card peeks 14px and settles — one 900ms hint that the gesture exists, shown once per session. Reduce Motion skips the peek.
The panel is colors.danger for an owner and textSecondary for Leave — the only filled destructive surface in the app. It is earned: swiping is cheap, so the reveal has to look like a live wire.
Motion
Every duration below is a constant already in the codebase.
M1
Screen entrance — header, stats, pill, tabs then sections rise 8px and fade, listRiseMs 150, 60ms stagger.
M2
Stat bloom — counts fade up on arrival, valueBloomMs 180.
M3
Tab swap — underline slides between tabs, underlineMs 200; label colour labelColorMs 150; panel rises 150.
M4
Section expand — body opens over 260ms, chevron rotates with it instead of snapping.
M5
Sheet — scrim scrimInMs 150, sheet rises 420px over sheetInMs 200; out at 150. Entries stagger 40ms behind it.
M6
Undo bar — the 2px line drains full width to zero over the 5s window, linear, so the remaining time is readable at a glance.
M7
Delete — the row collapses to nothing over 280ms rather than vanishing, so the list stays legible.
M8
Toast — in over SHOW_MS 180, out at 180. Plain toasts hold HOLD_MS 1600; the undo toast holds 5000. Undo restores the row in place, expanding it back over 260ms.
M9
Press feedback — 0.985 scale on kebabs, menu entries and CTAs, matching usePressFeedback.
Reduce Motion drops every rise, rotation and stagger and keeps the scrim fade, as BottomSheet already does.
Try it: kebab on a postcard or a published itinerary, then the destructive row — sheet → collapse → undo toast, one overlay the whole way. On Trips, drag a card left.
