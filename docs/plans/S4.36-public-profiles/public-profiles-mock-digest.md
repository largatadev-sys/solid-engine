# S4.36 — public-profiles mock digest *(source: the founder's Claude Design canvas "Public Profiles S4.36 Spec", delivered 2026-08-25; archived 1:1 in `mock-render.dc.html`, handoff in `mock-handoff.md`)*

The canvas is the **design baseline** (CLAUDE.md mock rule), superseding the spec's decision-5 "S4.21 frames + three deltas" — the founder supplied a dedicated canvas after all. Four frames: **1a** public profile (another traveler) · **1b** empty public profile · **1c** Discover suggestions with the People group · **1d** People results + no-results variant, plus contract cards C1–C7 and motion cards M1–M5. This digest records the load-bearing values read from the canvas's own markup, **and — critically — the boundary between what is S4.36's to build and what is story B's**, because the canvas deliberately draws both.

**Handling notes.** Handoff fidelity: high — colors, type, spacing, copy, and motion timings final. Frames are 393px; Inter throughout. Status bar is phone chrome, not built UI (the S4.21 digest's standing deviation). The interactive render needs the Claude Design dc runtime (`support.js`) beside the file; the markup is the normative artifact either way. Glyphs (`·`, `—`, `→`, `×`, `°`, `♥`) were restored from a transit encoding mangle; every **shipping copy string** is plain ASCII and was transcribed byte-for-byte.

**Frame 1d — People results.** Header: back chevron + the persistent field (unfocused: 1px #EBE9E2) holding the query. Count line **"{n} people"** 13/600 muted, updating with the query. **Motion contract for S4.36**: M2, M3, M4 adopted as written; **M5 (Reduce Motion) normative** — translates and staggers drop to 120ms opacity swaps, chevron rotation and press-scale removed, nothing conveys meaning through motion alone. From M1 only the press scale ships now.

## What this canvas draws that S4.36 does NOT build — named, so the frame is never read as scope

- **The Following state, C1's whole state machine, and M1's crossfade are STORY B's baseline, pre-answered here.** C1 (optimistic flip, background POST, revert + toast "Couldn't follow @handle", confirm-free unfollow, idempotent server-side) and C2's Following treatment (neutral outline + leading check) describe the real follow feature. S4.36's pill has exactly one state — Follow, filled terracotta — and its tap mutates nothing (spec decision 11). Story B inherits these cards as its opening design input.
- **The likes row on the postcard card ("♥ 31 likes") does not ship.** No real like exists (S4.4/S4.6 territory) and spec decision 6 forbids stub numbers in front of strangers. The public postcard card ships without the likes row; it returns when a real count exists. This is a deviation from the frame, stated per the mock rule rather than passed as a choice.
- **C7's "deactivated/blocked handle" wording maps to the 404 state.** No block or deactivation feature exists (account deletion is S5.5). S4.36 renders C7's copy — **"This profile isn't available"** — for the profile-read 404: unknown handle, renamed handle, un-onboarded account. The offline note (header from cache, lists show inline Retry) is adopted as written.

## Two canvas lines that were DECISIONS, not values — flagged at delivery, ruled the same day

1. **C4's self-routing contradicted spec decision 9, and the founder re-ruled in C4's favour** *(2026-08-25, recorded in the spec's Comments)*: viewing yourself — byline tap or direct address of your own handle — lands on the **own Profile tab**, never the public projection, which therefore never renders for its owner. The frames' Follow pill and back-chevron chrome are strictly another-traveler UI.
2. **C5's self-exclusion adopted and widened**: self never appears in People **suggestions or results**, enforced server-side. The founder's ground: you shouldn't be able to search yourself — other social platforms don't.

## Amended after the build — the stats row *(2026-08-26, founder at the build review)*

**C3's two-cell row is superseded: four cells ship** — Published · Postcards · Followers · Following. **The last two render `—`, not a number**, until story B. The canvas's line *"Follower counts are not public"* is overtaken by the founder's call that the slots should exist now, since following is the next story and a layout that moves twice is worse than a slot that waits. What the canvas got right and this amendment keeps: **no invented number reaches a stranger** — the stub metrics stay owner-only, which was the point C3's two-cell cut was serving. `Trips` remains off the row, unamended, because the owner's count includes private memberships. Full reasoning and the rejected alternatives are in the spec's Comments.
