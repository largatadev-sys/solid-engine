## Problem Statement

S4.39 built the whole of private profiles on the wire and not one screen, by design. Until the screens exist the feature is unreachable, and the shipped client is now wrong in five places at once: nothing can flip a profile; the Follow pill reads "Following" when the server answered `requested`; the published itinerary page still says "Follow, coming soon" in its creator row; the preview screen still offers a Private chip the server refuses by name; and a stranger who meets a `PROFILE_PRIVATE` refusal is shown a retry link, as if the server had failed. A private owner has no inbox to decide requests from and no way to remove a follower. The founder's driver is the one S4.39 recorded — a reason to open the app between trips — and none of it is usable until this story lands.

## Solution

Every screen the S4.39 contract implies, drawn by the founder's canvas and built to it. A private profile shows a non-follower its header, its four counts and one sealed-postcard notice, with the Follow pill as the only thing to tap; an approved follower sees the full profile. The pill gains a Requested state whose second tap cancels. The Account screen behind the cog gains a Private profile switch and, while private, a Follow requests row leading to a list with Approve and Decline on every row. The own Followers list gains a kebab that removes a follower behind a confirm. The published page's creator row gets the real pill. The retired audience control leaves the preview and the trip card, and the composer's privacy note goes. The owner's surfaces go live over the existing per-traveler topic; everyone else's stay focus-fresh. The two stories merge back to back, S4.39 first.

## User Stories

1. As a traveler, I want a Private profile switch on my Account screen, so that flipping my profile is one tap and not a form.
2. As a traveler flipping to private, I want the switch to save at once and revert with a toast if it fails, so that the screen never lies about my state.
3. As a traveler flipping to public, I want to be told first that everyone will see my postcards and that everyone who asked will be approved, so that the flip is never a surprise.
4. As a private traveler, I want a Follow requests row on my Account screen, so that I have one place to decide who is in.
5. As a public traveler, I want no Follow requests row at all, so that my Account screen does not offer a door onto an empty room.
6. As a private traveler, I want each request as a row with Approve and Decline, so that I decide per person without opening anything.
7. As a private traveler, I want the row to leave the list the moment I tap and come back with a toast if the server refused, so that the list is honest about what happened.
8. As a private traveler, I want to tap a request row and see that person's profile before deciding, so that a decision is informed.
9. As a private traveler, I want my requests list and my followers list to update while I am looking at them, so that a request that just arrived is not something I have to pull to refresh for.
10. As a traveler, I want a kebab on every row of my own Followers list that offers Remove follower behind a confirm, so that saying no is available on every profile, public or private.
11. As a traveler removing a follower, I want the confirm to say they will not be told and will have to follow me again, so that I know exactly what the act does before I do it.
12. As a stranger opening a private profile, I want to see the header and the four counts and one clear notice that the postcards are for approved followers, so that I know why the page ends where it does and what to do about it.
13. As a stranger on a private profile, I want nothing below the pill to be tappable, so that the page does not offer doors it cannot open.
14. As a stranger tapping Follow on a private profile, I want the pill to read Requested at once, so that I know I asked and that the owner decides.
15. As a requester, I want a second tap on Requested to cancel with no confirm, so that changing my mind costs one tap, like unfollow.
16. As a requester whose request was declined, I want the pill to read Follow again on my next visit with no announcement, so that a decline stays quiet.
17. As an approved follower of a private profile, I want the full profile with its tabs and postcards, so that being approved means something.
18. As a traveler reading a published itinerary, I want a real Follow pill in the creator row that behaves exactly as the profile's, so that admiring a plan can turn into following its author without leaving the page.
19. As the creator of a published itinerary, I want no pill on my own page, so that I am never offered to follow myself.
20. As a traveler who opens a diary from an old feed card after its author went private, I want the same notice with the author's name as the way to their profile, so that a dead end still leads somewhere.
21. As a traveler publishing an itinerary, I want the preview to say plainly that everyone on Largata can find and read it, with no Public/Private choice, so that a retired switch never reappears as a dead click.
22. As a traveler posting a postcard, I want the composer to stop telling me who can see it, so that it never tells me something that stopped being true.
23. As a traveler on a slow or failing connection, I want every optimistic change to revert with the canvas's toast, so that a failed request is never a silent lie.
24. As a traveler with Reduce Motion on, I want every transition on these screens to be a plain opacity swap, so that nothing conveys meaning through motion alone.
25. As the founder, I want the two stories on the wire and on the screens to merge back to back, so that `dev` never carries the bounded lie S4.39 decision 14 accepted for an interval that now need not exist.

## Implementation Decisions

*(Founder rulings, 2026-09-04 in grilling order and 2026-09-05 at canvas delivery; mechanics follow from them. Every wire change is additive within /v1 and inside ADR-030's envelope.)*

1. **Two branches, one contract, back to back.** S4.40 branches from S4.39's feature branch and opens its own PR to `dev`; S4.39's PR merges first and S4.40's immediately after, so the shipped-pill interval S4.39 decision 14 accepted never exists on `dev`. **S4.39 stays frozen** — reviewed, green, held. **S4.40 owns its own backend work**, and that work is exactly the two socket event types of decision 12 with their listener and ITs. **No count field is added anywhere** *(R3-Q1, R4-Q1: the founder asked what a pending count was for; it existed only to gate the going-public confirm, and the confirm now shows unconditionally)*.
2. **Design baseline = the founder's canvas**, adopted as drawn, its decision log accepted, its motion contract normative (decision 16). Where the canvas settled a treatment the grilling left open — the Account screen as rows only, dialog and sheet geometry, the compact pill's height, the "Asked {ago}" line, the destructive red, the toast wordings — the canvas rules (the digest lists them).
3. **The private profile as a non-follower sees it** *(frames 1a–1c; R1-Q3, R1-Q4, R1-Q5, R2-Q4; canvas items 1 and 3)*. The identity block unchanged and **without any chip** — the canvas dropped the Private chip the grilling had placed in the meta row (R2-Q4, R3-Q5), and the founder accepted it: the notice is the only statement of the state. **All four stat cells inert.** The pill is the only tappable thing on the screen. Where the tab row used to be, the **sealed-postcard notice** — "{First name}'s postcards are for approved followers." / "Follow to ask. {First name} decides who's in." — and **the notice is the last element**: no Diary tab, no Itineraries tab, and **no published showcase**. That last point reverses the grilling's Round 2 Q5, which had kept the showcase as a headed section to hold S4.39 decision 2 as written; the canvas hid it, the founder accepted, and the consequence named at the grilling stands and is accepted: **the Published cell is a number with no list behind it on this screen.** The itineraries stay exactly as public as ADR-034 says — in Discover, in search, and by address — so the wire is untouched and the glossary gains a parenthetical rather than an amendment. The same screen serves the `requested` viewer (1b) with the pill in its third state.
4. **The approved follower** *(frame 1d)* sees the full S4.37 profile: tabs, the Following pill, pressable Followers and Following cells, diary sections. **The Follows-you chip stays exactly as S4.37 ships it.** The canvas draws none on 1d and its decision log calls the chip anatomy "unused in this story"; that is adopted narrowly — 1d draws a subject who does not follow the viewer — and not as a retirement, which would be an S4.37 change nobody argued (Further Notes).
5. **The own Profile tab is unchanged** *(frame 2; R3-Q5 reversed by canvas item 1)*. No private mark on the own header. The owner reads their state on the Account screen and nowhere else.
6. **The pill's three states and its machine** *(R1-Q6; canvas C1, C2's treatments)*: `none` renders Follow (filled), `following` renders Following (outline, leading check), `requested` renders Requested (outline, muted label, no glyph); height 40, radius 999, no width change between states. **Optimistic in every direction**: the profile read carries `visibility`, so a tap from `none` predicts `requested` on a private target and `following` on a public one before the server answers; the follow call's `state` answer reconciles whatever it says. A second tap from `requested` cancels and from `following` unfollows, both through the one delete path, both confirm-free. Failure reverts and toasts — "Couldn't send a request to @handle", "Couldn't cancel the request", and S4.37's follow/unfollow toasts for the public case. Taps during an in-flight request are ignored. The existing follow-state module widens from a boolean to this three-state machine; the existing follow mutation returns the server's state instead of `void`.
7. **The published itinerary's creator row gets the real pill** *(frame 6; R1-Q7: "should act like the follow button in profile")*. The coming-soon "Follow" the row has shown since S4.36 retires, and `follow` leaves the coming-soon surfaces. **The pill's state comes from the creator's profile read by handle** through the existing public-profile query — no field is added to the published response, the same three-state machine drives both pills, and the read is cached per page view. The compact pill is 32px high in the same three treatments. **It is hidden, not disabled,** when the creator is the viewer (the own showcase route renders this same screen), while the profile read is loading, and when the read answers 404.
8. **The Account screen** *(frames 3a–3c; R1-Q8, R1-Q11, R2-Q8, R2-Q9, R2-Q11, R3-Q2, R4-Q1)* is **rebuilt to the frame**: a pushed screen titled "Account" with a back chevron and four rows — **Edit profile** · **Private profile** (switch, with the helper "Only followers you approve can see your postcards and who you follow. Your published itineraries stay public.") · **Follow requests**, **rendered only while the profile is private** · **Sign out**. **My Trips and Reload are removed**, and so are the wordmark, the "SIGNED IN" tagline and the identity card the current screen shows — the canvas draws rows only, and the Profile tab header shows identity one tap back. **The switch saves on flip** through the profile patch, optimistic, reverting with "Couldn't change your profile visibility" on failure. **Private → public always opens the confirm** ("Make your profile public?" / "Going public lets everyone see your postcards, and approves anyone who has asked to follow you." / Cancel · Go public) — unconditionally, with no lookup, which is what removed the need for a count. **Public → private has no confirm**: it takes nothing from anyone. Going public drops the Follow requests row with the row-exit motion. **No count and no badge on any row**, ever *(R3-Q6: "no dont show even on stats")*. The cost is on the record: with notifications parked, an owner learns of a request only by opening the list; the notifications backlog line already carries "request received" as its candidate event, and that remains the answer.
9. **The Follow requests list** *(frames 4a/4b; R1-Q8, R1-Q9, R2-Q7)* is a **new pushed screen in the Profile group**, opened from the Account row. It reads the inbox in cursor pages of 20, fetching five rows from the end (S4.37's list rule), **with no count line**. Each row is the list-row anatomy plus "Asked {ago}" and two trailing controls, **Approve** (filled) and **Decline** (text), keyed by the requester's traveler id as the contract says. **Both remove the row optimistically** and restore it with a toast on failure ("Couldn't approve @handle", "Couldn't decline"); reaching zero rows swaps in the empty state ("No requests right now" / "When someone asks to follow you, they'll show up here."). **Decline has no confirm** — it is silent and re-requestable, so nothing is lost. Row tap opens the requester's profile. The "Asked {ago}" line reuses the invitation card's ago grammar.
10. **Remove-follower on the own Followers list** *(frames 5a–5c; R1-Q10)*. On the **own followers list only**, a trailing kebab replaces the chevron on every row and opens a sheet with one destructive action, **Remove follower**, and Dismiss; the action opens the confirm ("Remove @handle?" / "They won't be told, and they'll have to follow you again." / Cancel · Remove) and Remove calls the removal endpoint. The row leaves with the row-exit motion and the count line updates. **No undo toast** — the owner cannot recreate an edge, so there is nothing to undo with. Public and private owners alike, per S4.39 decision 7. The own Following list and every other traveler's lists keep their plain S4.37 rows.
11. **Gated reads reached by address** *(frame 7; R4-Q2)*. On a locked profile nothing below the pill is tappable, so the lists and the diary are unreachable by tapping, but every route stays addressable: a feed card cached before the author went private, a shared link, or back-navigation into a diary the viewer had open. A `PROFILE_PRIVATE` refusal on the per-trip diary, on either list, or on the profile's diary read renders **the same sealed-postcard notice with the traveler's first name as the one tappable thing**, opening their profile where the pill lives. **No retry link** — retrying a refusal is wrong. The branch is on the error's `code`, never its status.
12. **Live over the socket, narrowly** *(R1-Q17: "topic and socket should be included"; R2-Q2, R2-Q3, R3-Q4)*. **Two payload-less event types on the existing `traveler:{id}` topic**, in ADR-030 amendment (B)'s grammar: **`follow-requests.changed`** — emitted to the target's topic when a request is created, cancelled, approved (including the batch approval at the flip to public) or declined — and **`followers.changed`** — emitted to the followee's topic when a Follow edge against them is created or removed, remove-follower included. Emission is after commit, off the S4.39 domain events, in the identity module's own topic listener (the invitation and join-queue topic classes are the precedent), and carries no payload because the audience of each frame is one traveler. On the client, two handlers join the existing delivery registry and **invalidate the requests list, the own followers list and the own stats**; reconnect marks them stale as it does today. **Pull stays pull**: the pill on another traveler's profile and on a published page revalidates on focus, which is how a requester learns of an approval. **S4.39 decision 12 is amended on the record** — its "both inboxes go live together or not at all" clause is retired, and its premise that the invitation inbox is pull today was already stale: `invitation.received` and `join-requests.changed` have ridden this same topic since S4.35 (Further Notes).
13. **The audience control retires from the client** *(R1-Q14; frame 8)*. The preview's Public/Private chips and blurb are replaced by one static line — "Everyone on Largata can find and read this itinerary." — above Publish Itinerary and Continue Editing. **Publishing sends no audience**; the contract publishes as today when the field is absent. The audience-typed plumbing goes with it: the audience helpers, the show-trip-to mutation and repository call (already caller-less), the audience the removal undo queue carries through republish, and the client's `Visibility`/`PublishAudience` types. The wire's constant `visibility` field is simply not read. **The trip card badge keeps "Published"** (S4.15 ruled publication a card fact) and loses its Private branch. The founder noted a later story will decouple a trip from an itinerary and revisit the badge there.
14. **The composer's privacy note is removed entirely** *(R3-Q3: "just remove this note")* — the component, its copy, and both of its mounts (the composer and the entry screen). The note claimed every Largata traveler could see a postcard, which stopped being true, and the founder chose silence over a branch. **No consent line on the Photo Dump** *(R1-Q13)* — the dump's rule is already that anyone on the trip sees what is added; this reverses S4.39 decision 10's "owed info line" on the record.
15. **Nothing else is marked.** No private marks on search results, list rows, feed bylines or discovery cards *(R1-Q15; a lock in rows would need a cross-surface wire field, and search presence is open by ADR-034)*. No "removed badge" — the S4.39 out-of-scope phrase named nothing; removal is silent and the owner's list simply drops the row *(R1-Q16)*. No counts, no badges *(decision 8)*.
16. **The motion contract is normative** *(founder, 2026-09-05)*: **M1** pill crossfade 160ms across all three states, press scale 0.96 at 120ms, reverts reuse it · **M2** list cascade, fade + 6px rise, 200ms, 40ms stagger, first 8 rows · **M3** row exit, fade + 6px drop 200ms, no stagger, the list closes the gap, a restored row replays M2, zero rows swaps in the empty state with M2 · **M4** sheet, scrim 160ms, slide 260ms cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1), dismiss 200ms, sheet rows press 0.98 · **M5** confirm dialog, scrim 160ms, card fade + scale 0.96 → 1 over 200ms, cancel 160ms, confirming dismisses first then M3 · **M6** switch track 160ms, knob 160ms cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1), a reverted save slides back the same way; every new control presses at 0.96 over 120ms · **Reduce Motion** — every change is a 120ms opacity swap, no translates, slides, scale, staggers or press scale. The digest carries the full values.
17. **Copy lives in one module.** Every string on the canvas's handoff card, byte-for-byte, in a plain `.ts` copy module that the screens and the Playwright specs both import (the S4.28 TSX-import trap); the first-name substitutions ride the existing first-name helper. The strings are ASCII.
18. **Types and codes, additive.** The client's `Me` gains `profileVisibility`; the profile patch gains the optional `profileVisibility`; the public profile gains `visibility` and `viewerRelation`; the follow call's answer and the follow-request card get their shapes; `PROFILE_PRIVATE` is branched on by code. **No client-side analytics** — the client's track call is a console log (S4.37 decision 10), and the measures S4.39 decision 18 names are carried by its five server-side events, which this story neither adds to nor changes.
19. **S4.39's gate ticket closes through this story.** Its interval look — the shipped pill against a curl-flipped traveler — closes by construction: the two PRs merge back to back and the interval never exists on `dev`. Its seeder run moves to this story's gate, where the seeders run once against the merged contract.

## API Contract

*(This story consumes S4.39's contract and adds only two socket frames. Every path is under `/v1`.)*

**Consumed, unchanged from the S4.39 contract**

| Endpoint | This story's use |
|---|---|
| `GET /me` · `PATCH /me` | the switch reads and writes `profileVisibility` |
| `GET /travelers/{handle}` | `visibility` predicts the pill's optimistic target; `viewerRelation` is the pill's state; read once more per published page for the creator |
| `POST /travelers/{travelerId}/follow` · `DELETE …/follow` | the pill's tap and its cancel/unfollow; the `state` answer reconciles the optimistic flip |
| `GET /travelers/{handle}/diary/trips` · `/followers` · `/following` · `GET /feed/postcards/trips/{id}/by/{authorId}` | `403 PROFILE_PRIVATE` renders the notice |
| `GET /me/follow-requests` · `POST …/{travelerId}/approve` · `POST …/{travelerId}/decline` | the requests list and its two row actions |
| `DELETE /me/followers/{travelerId}` | Remove follower |
| `POST /itineraries/{id}/publish` | sent with no `audience` |

**Added — socket, on the existing `traveler:{id}` topic** (ADR-030 as amended at S4.35)

| Event type | Emitted to | On | Payload |
|---|---|---|---|
| `follow-requests.changed` | the target traveler's topic | a Follow Request created, cancelled, approved (each row of a flip-to-public batch included) or declined | none |
| `followers.changed` | the followee's topic | a Follow edge against them created or removed, remove-follower included | none |

Both are ADR-030 amendment (B) events: their audience is one traveler, so they carry nothing, and REST remains the sole authority on what the subscriber may read. Clients that do not know the types ignore them (decision 5 of ADR-030).

## Testing Decisions

A good test here asserts **what a traveler sees and what the wire answered**, never the internals that produced it. The seams, proposed at /to-spec and pending the founder's confirmation:

- **End-to-end — the Playwright web suite against the preview container, the highest seam and the primary one.** Roles per the test-identity rule: **t1 = private owner, t2 = approved follower, t3 = stranger, t4 = requester** (t5 unused). The walks: t1 flips private on the Account screen and the Follow requests row appears · t3 opens t1's profile and sees the header, four inert cells, the pill and the notice, no tabs and no showcase · t3 taps Follow and reads Requested, taps again and reads Follow · t4 requests, t1's list shows the row, Approve removes it, t4's next visit reads Following with the full profile · Decline removes the row and t4's next visit reads Follow · t1 removes t2 through the kebab, sheet and confirm, the row leaves, t2's next visit reads Follow · t1 flips public through the confirm and the row disappears · the published page's creator row shows the real pill in each state and none on t1's own page · a per-trip diary opened by address as t3 shows the notice with the linked name · the preview footer has no chips and the static line · the composer has no note. Every string asserted from the shared copy module. Prior art: the follow, public-profile, publish and invitation-inbox specs.
- **Backend — the two frames, at the IT seam.** The recording fan-out asserts, for each of the S4.39 domain events, that exactly the right frame reaches exactly the right traveler's topic after commit, with a null payload, and that a no-op (a repeat follow, an idempotent delete) emits nothing. Prior art: the S4.35 Travelers-tab events IT. Sabotage-checked: drop one emit and watch its assertion go red.
- **Mobile — pure modules under Jest.** The three-state pill machine (optimistic target from `visibility`, cancel, in-flight swallowing, revert, reconciliation against the server's `state`); the Account rows rule (the requests row only while private; the confirm only on the flip to public); the locked-profile projection (what renders for `none`, `requested`, `following`); the two frame handlers in the delivery registry (frame → invalidations; prior art: the trip-events suite); the copy module. **Structural guards in the house style**: `follow` gone from the coming-soon surfaces; no audience chip or blurb on the preview; the notice mounted at its three hosts; the note component gone; the kebab only on the own followers list. Each sabotage-checked once. Prior art: the follow-state, public-profile, coming-soon and trip-card-anatomy suites.
- **The device rung.** The recorded Gradle fault still blocks device builds; the **LAN preview on the founder's phone** is the rung that reaches a real finger — the switch, the kebab, the sheet, the confirm and the pill's press scale are what it closes. Reduce Motion is checked on the web preview with the OS setting on.
- **Process gates:** one full Jest run before any push that adds a file under `src`; the Playwright list check after adding specs; backend counts read from the summary with `failsafe:verify` appended, never the exit code; one Maven run at a time; the seeders run once at the gate (decision 19).

- **Private marks in rows** — search, lists, feed bylines, discovery cards (decision 15).
- **The Private chip and the Follows-you chip on private profiles** — the first declined at the canvas; the second untouched, S4.37's as shipped (decision 4).
- **The invitation inbox's own socket work** — it already rides the traveler topic since S4.35; nothing here changes it.
- **A pending-request count on the wire** — removed from the plan at R4-Q1.
- **The dump consent line** — reversed (decision 14).
- **An outgoing-requests list** — S4.39 decision 13 stands; `viewerRelation: requested` on each profile is the requester's whole view.
- **The trip card badge beyond dropping its Private branch** — the founder named a later story that decouples a trip from an itinerary and will revisit it there.
- **Reopening the S4.39 PR** — frozen (decision 1).

## Comments

**Implementation, 2026-09-05 — where the build deviated from this spec, and what it found.**

- **Decision 12's "recording fan-out" is not a thing this repo has.** This spec and ticket 08 both named it as the IT shape; the backend has no such helper. `TravelersTabEventsIT` — the prior art both cited — opens a **real WebSocket against a live random-port server**, subscribes to the topic, drives the mutation through REST and reads frames off the wire. `FollowTopicEventsIT` is built on that, unchanged in substance: same base, same rigs, the same `payload.isNull()` assertion, and `receivedNothingWithin` for the negative cases.
- **The cancellation event ticket 08 said to raise already had a home.** S4.39's `cancel` was silently idempotent and emitted nothing at all, not even analytics. Rather than mint a domain event, the broadcast rides its existing found-a-pending branch, so a cancel that cancelled nothing still says nothing. `grantTheEdge` turned out to be the single choke point for every edge born of a request, which is why approve and the flip-to-public batch both announce a followers change with no second call site.
- **The rig does not onboard, and the follow routes require it.** Every traveler in the new IT is onboarded explicitly after `travelerWithHandle`; without it all eight tests fail with `TRAVELER_NOT_FOUND`, which reads as a routing fault rather than a fixture one.

It returns the relation both calls actually settle to, and the machine reconciles from that.

- **Not closed by this build, and named rather than assumed:** the device rung (the recorded Gradle fault, four stories running now), the LAN-phone pass, Reduce Motion against a real OS setting, and the seeders against the merged contract. Ticket 09 carries all four.
