# S4.40 — private-profiles mock digest *(source: the founder's Claude Design canvas "Private Profiles S4.40 Spec", delivered 2026-09-05; archived 1:1 in `mock-render.dc.html`, handoff in `mock-handoff.md`)*

The canvas is the **design baseline** (CLAUDE.md mock rule) for everything S4.40 ships, and the direct successor of the S4.36 and S4.37 canvases whose anatomy it reuses verbatim. Sixteen frames: **1a** the private profile as a stranger sees it · **1b** the same in the Requested state · **1c** the same with nothing published · **1d** the same as an approved follower · **2** the own Profile tab header · **3a** Account, private · **3b** Account, public · **3c** the going-public confirm · **4a** the Follow requests list · **4b** its empty state · **5a** the own Followers list with a kebab per row · **5b** the row sheet · **5c** the remove confirm · **6** the published itinerary's creator row in all three pill states · **7** a gated read reached by address · **8** the itinerary preview footer — plus contract cards C1–C4, motion cards M1–M6 with a normative Reduce Motion card, a "Not drawn, by decision" card, and a handoff card carrying every value and copy string. This digest records the load-bearing values read from the canvas's own markup, and the boundary between what the frames draw and what this story builds.

**Handling notes.** Handoff fidelity: high — colors, type, spacing, copy and motion timings final. Frames are 393px; Inter throughout. Status bar is phone chrome, not built UI (the standing S4.21 deviation). Glyphs (`·`, `—`, `–`, `→`) were restored from the same transit encoding mangle the S4.36 and S4.37 digests record; every **shipping copy string** is plain ASCII and was transcribed byte-for-byte. The interactive render needs the Claude Design dc runtime (`support.js`) beside the file, which is not committed; the markup is the normative artifact either way. **The prototype is live in five places** — the pill on 1a/1b, the switch and confirm on 3a, Approve/Decline on 4a, the kebab → sheet → confirm → row exit on 5a, and the three compact pills on 6 — so the motion cards can be watched rather than read.

## What S4.40 builds from this canvas

**Frames 1a–1c — the private profile, not approved.** Pushed screen: 36px back target + "Profile" 15/700. Identity block = the S4.36 anatomy verbatim (72px avatar r36, #FDE4CF fallback with #C2410C initials, name 22/800, meta `@handle · #N` 13 muted, bio 13.5 #44403C) — **no chip of any kind**. Four stat cells in the S4.37 row, **all four inert** (no press state, no destination). The pill spans the content column: height 40, radius 999, 13.5/700, **no width change between states** — **Follow** filled #EA580C white · **Requested** white, 1px #E7E5E4, muted #78716C label, no glyph · **Following** white, 1px #E7E5E4, ink label, leading 14px check. Where the tab row used to be, the **notice**: 48px #FFF7ED circle holding the sealed-postcard glyph (paths in the markup), title **"Maya's postcards are for approved followers."** 15/700, support **"Follow to ask. Maya decides who's in."** 13 muted max-width 240. Nothing in the notice is tappable. **The notice is the last element** — no tabs, no published showcase, on all three frames (1c differs from 1a only in the Published cell reading 0). Failure toasts in the S4.37 dark-pill grammar (#1C1917, white 11/600, bottom, 2.5s): **"Couldn't send a request to @handle"** · **"Couldn't cancel the request"**.

**Frame 1d — approved follower.** The full S4.37 profile: tabs, the Following pill, pressable Followers/Following cells, the diary sections. No chip drawn — see the boundary section below for what that does and does not mean.

**Frame 2 — own Profile tab header.** Unchanged from S4.37: cog, stats, Edit Profile pill. **No private mark on the own header.** The owner's state is visible on the Account screen only.

**Frames 3a–3c — Account.** A pushed screen titled "Account" with the 36px back target — rows only, no wordmark, no identity card. Rows: min-height 52, label 15/600, hairline #E7E5E4 between rows — **Edit profile** (chevron #D6D3D1) · **Private profile** (switch 44×26, knob 20, off #D6D3D1 / on #EA580C) with helper 12.5 muted, max-width 300: **"Only followers you approve can see your postcards and who you follow. Your published itineraries stay public."** · **Follow requests** (chevron, **rendered only while private**) · **Sign out** (#B91C1C). **No count and no badge on any row.** Off → on saves at once. On → off opens the confirm (3c): dialog r20, title **"Make your profile public?"** 17/800, body **"Going public lets everyone see your postcards, and approves anyone who has asked to follow you."** 13.5 #44403C, buttons **Cancel** (outline, h40) / **Go public** (filled, h40). Going public drops the Follow requests row with M3. Failure toast: **"Couldn't change your profile visibility"**.

**Frames 4a/4b — Follow requests.** Pushed screen "Follow requests". **No count line.** Row = the list-row anatomy (44px avatar r22, name 15/700, handle 13 muted) plus a third line **"Asked 2d ago"** 12 muted, and two trailing controls: **Approve** (h36, filled #EA580C, white 13/700, padding 0 16) and **Decline** (text button, muted 13/700, hover #F5F5F4). Row tap opens the requester's profile. Empty (4b): 48px cream circle with the person-plus glyph, **"No requests right now"** / **"When someone asks to follow you, they'll show up here."** Failure toasts: **"Couldn't approve @handle"** · **"Couldn't decline"**.

**Frames 5a–5c — own Followers.** The S4.37 list with a trailing **kebab** (three dots, 36px target, hover #F5F5F4) replacing the chevron; the count line **"{n} followers"** 13/600 muted updates live. Sheet (5b): r24 top corners, grab handle, an identity header (36px avatar, name 14/700, handle 12 muted) over a hairline, then two 52px rows — **Remove follower** (#B91C1C) and **Dismiss**. Confirm (5c): **"Remove @handle?"** / **"They won't be told, and they'll have to follow you again."**, buttons **Cancel** / **Remove** (filled #B91C1C, pressed #991B1B). The row leaves with M3. **No undo toast.**

**Frame 6 — the creator row on a published itinerary.** Avatar 36 r18, name 14/700, handle 12 muted, and the **compact pill**: height 32, 13/700, padding 0 14, check 12px, the same three treatments and the same C1 machine. **Hidden when the creator is the viewer.** The frame draws the three states side by side; the shipped row shows one.

**Frame 7 — a gated read by address.** The per-trip diary opened from a feed card cached before the author went private, a shared link, or back-navigation. Header: back + the trip title. Body replaced by the notice; **only "Maya" in the title is tappable** (#C2410C, underline at 35% alpha, offset 3px) and opens her profile. **No retry link.** The same block, un-linked, is what the profile's own notice is.

**Frame 8 — the preview footer.** The Public/Private audience chips and their blurb are **removed**. One static line **"Everyone on Largata can find and read this itinerary."** 13 muted, centered, above **Publish Itinerary** (h44 filled) and **Continue Editing** (h44 outlined).

**Contracts.** **C1** — states none / requested / following; a tap on a private profile goes to Requested optimistically, on a public one to Following; a second tap cancels or unfollows, confirm-free; failure reverts and toasts; taps during an in-flight request are ignored. **C2** — Approve and Decline remove the row optimistically; failure restores it and toasts; Decline has no confirm and is silent to the requester. **C3** — the switch saves on flip, optimistic, reverting with a toast; private → public passes through 3c, public → private has no confirm. **C4** — the requests list, the own followers list and the own stats update without a refresh; the pill on other travelers' screens refreshes on focus.

**Motion contract** *(the founder, 2026-09-05: "take note of the motion contract")*: **M1** pill crossfade — fill, border and label 160ms ease across all three states, press scale 0.96 at 120ms, reverts reuse it (S4.37's M1, widened) · **M2** list cascade — fade + 6px rise, 200ms ease, 40ms stagger, first 8 rows (S4.37's M2) · **M3** row exit — fade + 6px drop over 200ms ease, no stagger, then the list closes the gap; a failed request replays M2 on the restored row; reaching zero rows swaps in the empty state with M2 · **M4** sheet — scrim 160ms ease, sheet slides up 260ms cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1), dismiss reverses at 200ms, sheet rows press at 0.98 · **M5** confirm dialog — scrim 160ms, card fades and scales 0.96 → 1 over 200ms cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1), cancel reverses at 160ms, confirming dismisses first and then the row plays M3 · **M6** switch and press — track color 160ms ease, knob 160ms cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1), a reverted save slides it back the same way; every new control presses at 0.96 over 120ms, hover/press fills 160ms · **Reduce Motion, normative** — every state change becomes a 120ms opacity swap; no translates, slides, scale, staggers or press scale; sheets and dialogs simply fade.

## What this canvas draws that S4.40 does NOT build — named, so the frame is never read as scope

- **Frame 1d draws no Follows-you chip, and this story does not retire it.** The decision log's item 2 reads "No Follows-you chip on 1d for the same reason; the chip anatomy is unused in this story", and the "Not drawn" card says no Follows-you chip on any header. The reason given — "the notice already says it" — is a reason against the Private chip, not against a chip that says something else entirely. S4.37's C5 chip is shipped behaviour with its own ruling and its own tests, and nobody argued its retirement at this grilling. **Adopted narrowly:** 1d draws a subject who does not follow the viewer, so no chip appears; a private profile that does follow the viewer still shows the chip exactly as S4.37 ships it. Retiring it is an S4.37 change, flagged to the founder in the spec's Further Notes, and takes a ruling of its own.
- **The row hover states** (`#FAFAF9`, `#F5F5F4`) are web-prototype affordances; what ships is the press treatment the S4.37 digest records.
- **The sample travelers** (Diego Alonte, Noor Bautista, Jeb Guardiario, Amara Chen, Lena Okafor, Tomas Rivera) are prototype data. Every walk and write-up keeps the pool's self-identifying names, per the test-identity rule.
- **Frame 6 is a contract card in the shape of a row**: three pills side by side. The shipped creator row carries one pill in one state.
- **The handoff's "Codebase touchpoints" and "Tests worth extending" lists** are the canvas author's guess at the tree, not the ticket plan; the tickets name the modules.

## Canvas lines that were decisions

The canvas arrived after the grilling closed and **changed three things the grilling had ruled**, which the founder accepted at delivery ("i made a few changes with the design. these are all good", 2026-09-05). Each is recorded in the spec as a ruling with its consequence named:

1. **No Private chip anywhere** (decision-log item 1) — reverses Round 2 Q4's chip and Round 3 Q5's chip on the own tab. The notice is the only statement of the profile's state to a non-follower; the owner reads their own state on the Account screen.
2. **The published showcase is hidden on a private profile a non-follower views** (item 3) — reverses Round 2 Q5, where option (a) kept the showcase as a headed section precisely to hold S4.39 decision 2 and the glossary as written. The consequence the grilling named stands and is accepted: the Published cell is a number with no list behind it on that screen. The itineraries themselves remain exactly as public as ADR-034 says — in Discover, in search, and by address — so the wire is untouched and the glossary gains a parenthetical rather than an amendment.
3. **Item 4's open question is closed: the helper text stays.** "Your published itineraries stay public." remains true — they do, everywhere but the locked profile page — so it is kept verbatim.
4. **M3–M6 and the Reduce Motion card** (item 5) are adopted as written; M1 and M2 are S4.37's, widened to the third pill state and the new lists.

Everything else the canvas draws matches a recorded ruling: the sealed-postcard notice (R2-Q4) · tabs gone, nothing tappable but the pill (R1-Q3, R1-Q5) · inert cells (R1-Q4) · the Requested treatment and confirm-free cancel (R1-Q6) · the compact pill behaving as the profile's (R1-Q7) · requests behind the cog, a list like Followers, no count (R1-Q8, R3-Q6) · Approve/Decline per row, no decline confirm (R2-Q7, R1-Q9) · kebab, sheet, confirm, no undo (R1-Q10) · the switch on the Account screen saving on flip (R1-Q11, R2-Q8) · the always-on going-public confirm with no lookup (R4-Q1) · My Trips and Reload gone (R2-Q11) · the Follow requests row only while private (R3-Q2) · no composer note (R3-Q3) · no dump line (R1-Q13) · the Published badge kept, the chips gone (R1-Q14) · no marks in rows (R1-Q15) · the gated read's linked name and no retry (R4-Q2) · no empty showcase section (R4-Q3). What the canvas newly *settles* are treatments the rulings left open — the Account screen's rows-only shape with no identity card, the dialog and sheet geometry, the compact pill's 32px height, the "Asked {ago}" line, the destructive red, and the toast wordings — all adopted as drawn.

## Superseded on the rung — three boundaries the founder moved after this digest was written *(2026-09-05)*

**This digest is an archive: everything above records what the canvas said on 2026-09-05, and none of it is edited after the fact.** What follows is what the founder changed while walking the built screens on a real phone, so a later reader is not misled by a baseline the product has deliberately left behind. Each is argued in the spec's Comments.

- **The Follows-you chip IS retired** — reversing the "and this story does not retire it" line above. That line was correct when written: the canvas implied the retirement without arguing it, so the spec adopted the narrow reading and flagged the question. The founder answered it on the rung — *"remove the follows you pill"* — and the chip is gone from every header, public and private. The wire keeps `followsViewer` (ADR-008 additivity).

- **The remove-follower sheet (frame 5b) drops its identity header and its Dismiss row.** The sheet's own title already names the subject, so the identity block said it twice; and a scrim tap has always dismissed, so the row offered nothing new. What ships is the handle as title and one destructive row.

- **The Follow requests row (frame 4a) leads with the HANDLE, drops the display name, and shrinks Approve from h36 to h28.** The frame draws a 44px avatar, three text lines and two h36 controls; measured on a 393px viewport that leaves ~127px for the text, so the "Asked 2d ago" line was clipped away entirely and the row read as though it had no ago at all. Shipped: handle on the first line, the ago beneath it, the display name kept only in the avatar's accessibility label, and both controls at the smaller chip size with `hitSlop` holding their touch targets. **The general lesson, worth more than the change:** a canvas frame is wider than a phone, so any row this dense has to be measured on a real viewport before its anatomy is trusted.
