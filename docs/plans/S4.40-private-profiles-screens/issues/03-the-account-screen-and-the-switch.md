# 03 — The Account screen and the visibility switch

**What to build:** the Account screen behind the cog, rebuilt to the canvas as rows, carries a **Private profile** switch that saves on flip. Going private just saves; going public asks first, always. A refused save slides the switch back and says so. My Trips, Reload, the wordmark, the tagline and the identity card go (spec decision 8; canvas frames 3a–3c, contract C3, motion M5 and M6). The Follow requests row lands with ticket 04.
