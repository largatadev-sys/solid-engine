Decided — S4.0 ruled it, the build follows the ruling

Nine places where the running app deliberately departs from the drawing. Each has a numbered decision in docs/plans/S4.0-auth-onboarding-retrofit/spec.md. Nothing here needs a new call — it needs the mock updated so the next reader doesn’t re-open a settled question.

PinDrawnBuiltRuling
1 Landing carries the sign-in form inline — one screen does branding, email/password, social and the sign-up link. Three screens: welcome (wordmark + two buttons) → sign-in / sign-up. Decision 1. The flow is welcome → sign-up (or sign-in) → verify → four steps → complete.
2 “Sign in with Apple”, black, above the Google button. Absent. Decision 3 + ADR-010. Apple’s mandate applies only on iOS, and the alpha is Android-only. Ships at the iOS activation.
3 Sign-up has no social buttons at all — email/password only. Google doorway renders on both sign-up and sign-in. Decision 3. The wireframes omit Google; the flow gains it, reusing S0.5/S0.6 machinery unchanged.
4 “By continuing, you agree to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.” Absent. Decision 9. No consent line until the documents exist — a link to nothing is worse than no line. Pre-alpha trigger.
5 Avatar with a camera badge and “Upload Photo” beneath it. Avatar only — Google photo, or initials. Decision 8. The affordance is absent, not disabled, until the media pipeline lands at S3.3. The S1.3 dead-click lesson as law.
6 Step 2 of 4 → Step 3 of 4 → Step 3 of 4 → Step 3 of 4 → Step 4 of 4. Five screens, four labels, three collisions. profile 1 · goals 2 · interests 3 · travel-setup 4. Verification sits before the count; completion sits outside it. Decision 1 resolves it explicitly — the wireframe’s inconsistency is named in the spec. Re-confirmed by the founder 2026-08-04, matching the build exactly.
7 Black CTAs at 4px radius; a single orange #FF751F on “Forgot password?”. Terracotta #D96C4A CTAs at 12px radius; the same terracotta as the only accent. Decision 10 / ADR-016. Explicitly “supersedes the 07/18 mock’s orange as brand evidence”. Note the CTA changes shape, not just colour.
8 Completion is icon + two lines + one button. Same, plus a summary card listing what was set up. Decision 6. The summary exists but “claims only true things” — goals and interests currently have no reader, and no line may imply otherwise.
9 “Earn from my itineraries” as the fifth goal. Present, stored, and analytics-measured. Decision 5 narrows the 2026-07-17 cut rather than voiding it: it ships as signal-gathering, carrying no product promise.

Drift — settled by the founder, 2026-08-04 → S4.12

Five differences that traced to no ruling in the spec, the ADR log, or the epic map. All five are now decided and specced as docs/plans/S4.12-auth-onboarding-fidelity/. Four resolved toward the frame, one toward the copy. No backend change in any of them.

PinDrawnBuiltRuling
10 “Plan less. Experience more.” “Plan together. Travel better.” — welcome.tsx:17 Adopt the frame. The mock’s tagline ships. Two versions were in circulation on the first screen anyone sees; the founder settled it toward the drawing.
11 party-popper in the completion circle. sparkle — complete.tsx:56 Adopt the frame. The standing mock rule, and the glyph already exists. sparkle has one consumer, so it leaves with the change rather than lingering dead.
12 An eye icon inside the password field. The words “Show” / “Hide”. Adopt the frame. The eye ships on both auth screens; the “Show password” / “Hide password” accessibility labels stay, so nothing is lost to a screen reader.
13 “Forgot password?” right-aligned, bold, accent-coloured, inside the credentials block. Centred, below the Sign In button. Adopt the frame’s placement. Right-aligned under the password field. The frame expressed this on a landing that no longer exists — the placement carries over, the screen doesn’t.
14 “Select all that apply” — implies zero is a valid answer. Continue disabled until one goal is picked. Adopt the copy. The minimum goes; zero becomes valid. This one has a consequence — see below.

The consequence of pin 14 — a trap no straight-through test can see

Dropping the goals minimum breaks the resume gate, and it is worth stating on its own because it is the only place in this story where a copy decision reaches into behaviour.

MechanismSymptomResolution
! The gate resolves “where does this traveller resume?” by asking each step whether its data is absent — handle === null, country === null, and goals.length === 0. Once empty is a legal answer, that predicate can no longer tell not asked from asked, chose none. Pick nothing, force-quit, relaunch — back on the goals step, every cold start. Goals leaves the resume ladder. Still in the forward walk; an interrupted traveller resumes at interests. Costs one skipped optional question in the interrupted case. The nullable-goals alternative is truthful but is a column-semantics change for a step nothing reads — recorded, rejected.

The drawing contradicts itself — resolve before it becomes the baseline again

Three places where the export disagrees with the export. The build already picked a side in each; these are flagged so the next reconciliation doesn’t treat the losing side as a missed detail.

PinOn one frameOn anotherWhere it lands
15 Landing inputs: 1px #F7F7F7 border, 12px radius — nearly borderless. Sign-up, profile and travel-setup inputs: 1px #8F8F8F, 4px radius. Same control, two treatments, two frames apart. The build runs one FormField, so it had to choose; the mock should say which.
16 Google button: 8px radius with a drop shadow. Every other control on the board: 4px, no shadow. Probably an unmodified vendor component pasted in. Google’s brand guidelines do constrain this button — if that’s the reason, it’s a legitimate exception worth stating.
17 Goal rows declare height: 50px. …with padding: 20px around a 32px icon — 72px of content in a 50px box. Figma clips it silently. The shipped token is controls.optionHeight = 72, which is what the content actually needs — the build is right and the frame is wrong.

Where this landed — S4.12

  1. Nothing structural. All eight screens exist and route in the shipped order. No missing screen, no missing field — the retrofit already happened at S4.0.
  2. Two of the founder’s three rulings described the build as it already stands, and are recorded so they aren’t raised a third time. “Make sure the theme is applied” — already true: every .tsx and .ts under mobile/ scanned, zero colour literals outside tokens.ts. “Step 1 after verification through step 4 setup profile, last screen no step” — already true, exactly, and shipped as S4.0 decision 1. Both were prompted by this render drawing the mock’s labels faithfully; the correction was to the drawing.
  3. The five drift items are specced as docs/plans/S4.12-auth-onboarding-fidelity/ — three tickets, no blocking edges between them, no backend change in any of them.
  4. The mock gets annotated, not edited. The step-label collisions, the two input treatments and the clipped option rows stay in the file — with a header above it naming every element the build overrules and why. A superseded mock with no annotation misleads with authority, which is what cost this session an afternoon.

Rendering note, stated rather than passed off as a choice: the frames specify Inter, and the artifact sandbox blocks font CDNs. These render in the system UI face at Inter’s metrics. Everything else — sizes, weights, spacing, radii, colour — is taken from the export verbatim, and the “Largata theme” values from mobile/src/theme/tokens.ts.
